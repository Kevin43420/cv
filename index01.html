ABOUT ME

About Me

Observing the world through the lens, expressing thought through image

Image Is Language

Since 2005, I have worked as a news photographer. My work has been published in more than 300 media outlets worldwide, with over 1,200 images published, including covers for Caijing and Nanfang Daily.

Since May 2021, I have independently created the documentary feature 150000 Shots, completed after three years of work, shortlisted at multiple international film festivals and awarded the Remi Silver Award at the Houston International Film Festival. In September 2026, two of my photographs were selected for the Shanghai Leica Festival and displayed on the large screen at Shanghai West Bund.

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Art Tech Business at the School of Business, Lingnan University, exploring the intersection of art and technology.

2005 — 2008

Freelance Photographer · Photo Editor

News Documentary Photography

2008: Invited by China Merchants Bank, I traveled to Yunnan, Hunan, Heilongjiang, Chongqing, and Shanxi for teaching support in rural schools, staying one week in each location. During the visits I photographed thousands of documentary images, later compiled into an internal publication. I also won the Nikon Annual Photography Competition award.

2007: As a freelance photographer, I shot reporting and news images for domestic and international media, including Three-Body Life Weekly, China News Weekly, China Youth Daily, New Century Weekly, Oriental Morning Post, and The Beijing News, as well as agencies such as Xinhua, Reuters, and CCTV’s Morning News. I published over 1,200 works across more than 300 magazines, newspapers, agencies, and TV stations worldwide, including covers for Caijing and Southern Metropolis Daily.

Late 2006: Worked as an image editor at Brand magazine under Southern Weekly, responsible for cover image selection, section planning, and commissioned assignments. Later, I joined the South China Advertising Department of Reference News as a photographer and photo editor.

2005–Early 2006: Worked at Shenzhen Phototex Photo Agency as Chief Photographer and Photo Editor, coordinating 2,000+ photographers and several hundred media outlets. I represented the company at the 2006 First Lianzhou International Photography Festival and the 2006 Pingyao International Photography Festival.
